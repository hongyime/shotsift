## Notes

- **Blink detection** is Stage II (needs 3DDFA landmarks). For the video, the
  "blink rejected" beat can be shown as a soft-focus reject instead — do NOT
  claim the prototype detects blinks.
- **Taste model**: show the training pipeline as "coming in Stage II," not a result.
- Record screen at 60fps (OBS), shoot with a second camera, sync with a clap.
- Voiceover on a lav/USB mic, burn in captions (judges watch muted).
- Music: YouTube Audio Library only.
- Upload as **Unlisted** ("Anyone with the link"). Verify in incognito before submitting.

## If time runs short

The hero demo (0:45–1:10) is non-negotiable. Cut in this order:
1. Natural-language search (not in prototype yet anyway)
2. The 2:00–2:15 taste-model slide (keep the one-line version)
3. B-roll montages — keep only the midnight-culling opener
